Integer column world for the forest greybox.

Heights, surface materials, and scatter are functions of (FOREST_WORLD_SEED, x, z). The arithmetic is wrapping integers. Species and yaw come from astraweave_core::sim::SimRng. Nothing in this module iterates a HashMap, and nothing calls libm.
