## Legacy loose fonts

15 of the 16 original TTF blobs under `assets/Other/Fonts/` embedded CC BY-SA 3.0 (the sixteenth, Kenney Space, embeds CC0). This field alone does not rule out a second license from the same author: Kenney Mini Square Mono is byte-identical to the one in the [explicitly CC0 official Kenney Fonts download](https://kenney.nl/assets/kenney-fonts) despite its legacy field. The checked ZIP SHA-256 is `4e69a86eef3cd47e9d8207413868cd08bcddeb2dae4047dbd10362e2a7a16bac`.

- Use that archive's 12 TTFs byte-for-byte (11 replacements and the existing Mini Square Mono), retain the independently CC0 Kenney Space file, and preserve the publisher notice bytes. The stored 564-byte notice matches the archive-member SHA-256 independently recorded in [the review](https://github.com/lazyxeon/AstraWeave/pull/790#discussion_r4724306636): `98402691e89c968502be5d2392c0de86bc787dd49f961fbb799e22c3bbb434e2`. The notice establishes the CC0 grant for the matching TTF files even where embedded metadata still lists CC BY-SA 3.0.
- Remove the three unmatched legacy TTFs (Bold, Future Square, and Thick) and the two unmatched webfont archives. The latter mix generated webfonts and specimen content, and the official TTF download does not establish rights for those exact archives. Kenney's [own historical OpenGameArt upload](https://opengameart.org/content/kenney-fonts) is CC0, but no removed file was an exact match to that download. An alternate version of a family existing under CC0 isn't proof about these files.
- No active consumer was found for the five removed paths or loose font directory. The in-engine default fonts come through the UI dependency and are unaffected. The live release packlists contain none of the loose files, so this change does not require repacking or new pins.

## Admission rule

Use the actual version and bytes, not a name, label, free price, or provider-wide assumption. Keep the rights grant, asset-to-source link and pin together. When an original page is gone, preserve a working archive capture that names the actual version. Missing evidence means unverified, not automatically prohibited. Keep assets with unresolved terms out of distribution unless there is adequate evidence for the exact version. Audit records, engine code, third-party software dependencies, and first-party content are not interchangeable with third-party art.
